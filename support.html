常見問題

出生地是怎麼抽的？
依照世界銀行公布的各國人口與出生率，算出每個國家每年的出生人數，再按比例隨機抽出。所以出生在印度或奈及利亞的機會，遠比出生在冰島高。

數據從哪裡來？
世界銀行《世界發展指標》（CC BY 4.0）。台灣不在世界銀行資料中，使用內政部、主計總處與 IMF 公開資料的近似值。詳細清單在 App 的「設定與說明」裡。

我的紀錄會不見嗎？
紀錄只存在你的手機上。刪除 App 會一併刪除紀錄。

FAQ

How is the birthplace drawn?
Each country's yearly births are computed from World Bank population and birth-rate data, and one is drawn in proportion. You're far more likely to be born in India or Nigeria than in Iceland.

Where does the data come from?
The World Bank's World Development Indicators (CC BY 4.0). Taiwan isn't covered by the World Bank, so approximate figures from Taiwan's Ministry of the Interior, DGBAS and the IMF are used. The full list is in Settings & About in the app.

Can I lose my history?
Your lives are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.
